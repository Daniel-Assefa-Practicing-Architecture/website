Students need both silence and company. The University of Gondar library shaped how Daniel Assefa Practicing Architecture designs for that double life: carrels that protect concentration, and a commons that lets ideas bump into each other.

Climate control for rare collections sat next to everyday comfort. Acoustic separation was as important as the atrium photograph.

If your campus in Ethiopia is growing, start with how people already study, under trees, on stairs, in borrowed rooms, and let the building become a more generous version of that habit.
