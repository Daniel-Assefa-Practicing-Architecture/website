A church is never only structure. It is a ledger of voices, feasts, and quiet Tuesdays. When the practice restores or expands a sanctuary, the first drawing is not a plan, it is a listening.

In Gojam, that meant sitting with craftspeople who still knew older joinery. In Addis Ababa, it meant finding a way for a growing congregation to gather without erasing the stone that already held their prayers.

The studio looks for three things:

- **Light that feels like welcome**, not spectacle.
- **Air that moves**, so a long liturgy does not become a trial.
- **Details a child can remember**, a threshold, a courtyard tree, a bell that still belongs to the street.

The portfolio on this site holds those projects. If you are a committee beginning a conversation, write to the Addis Ababa studio with the story of the place, not only the square metres.
