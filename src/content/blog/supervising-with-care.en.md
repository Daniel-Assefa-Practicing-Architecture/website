Drawings are promises. Supervision is how those promises stay kind on a dusty site.

At Daniel Assefa Building Consultant PLC the studio mentored eight junior architects and learned that a well-run site is not louder, it is clearer. Delivery improved by making decisions earlier, not by asking people to hurry.

What the practice still carries:

1. Walk the site as if your family will use the building.
2. Write notes that a contractor can act on before noon.
3. Protect the budget without humiliating the craft.

Daniel Assefa Practicing Architecture continues this work from Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, serving clients across the country who want that same temperament.
